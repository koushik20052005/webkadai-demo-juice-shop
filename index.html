Why Anna Nagar squeezes with us

Pressed, never poured

Slow cold-press machines keep the enzymes and flavour alive. No concentrates, no bottled mixes — ever.

Zero added sugar

Fruit is sweet enough. If a blend needs a lift, we use honey or dates — and we always tell you first.

Market-fresh, daily

Our fruit lands from Koyambedu market every morning at 5 AM. Yesterday's stock never sees today's glass.

Combos that save you money

Pre-built trays for gym mornings, office breaks and family evenings.

Signature of the season

Alphonso season is short, so we make it count. Our Mango Madness smoothie bowl — thick-blended alphonso, coconut flakes and chia — is the one regulars drive across the city for.
